About me

Qualified, experienced and here to listen.

  • Registered with the BACP
  • Level 5 Diploma in Psychotherapeutic Counselling Headstrong Academy
  • Diploma in Therapeutic Counselling Headstrong Academy
  • Intensive training in Couples Counselling

I'm Marsha Hunter, a qualified and experienced counsellor, registered with the BACP, with a passion for creating a safe space for both individuals and couples.

I obtained my Diploma in Therapeutic Counselling, followed by an advanced Level 5 Diploma in Psychotherapeutic Counselling at Headstrong Academy. I have also undertaken intensive training in Couples Counselling, deepening my understanding of the complexities of relationships, affairs and betrayal, separation, the processes of rebuilding trust, repairing relationships and strengthening communication, and GSRD (gender, sexuality and relationship diversity) relationships.

I create a welcoming and non-judgemental space where individuals and couples can feel heard and work through challenging feelings and situations.

My approach

Relational and kind.

You can bring the issues that feel relevant and important to you. Whether you're navigating relationship difficulties, facing personal challenges or just wanting to understand yourself better, my door is open to you.

Individual counselling

A space that is yours, to explore what you're facing at your own pace and to understand yourself better.

  • Personal challenges
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Understanding yourself

Couples counselling

Support for both of you to feel heard, to work through what has happened and to find a way forward.

  • Affairs and betrayal
  • Rebuilding trust
  • Strengthening communication
  • Repairing relationships
  • Separation
  • Gender, sexuality and relationship diversity
I don't believe therapy should be a space where you talk to someone who simply sits back and listens. I believe that, as a therapist, it is my job to explore your experiences together, with curiosity, openness and without judgement.
